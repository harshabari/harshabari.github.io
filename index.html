Proven Methodology

Industry-Ready Engineering Workflow

A structured, 7-phase engineering workflow ensuring design intent, manufacturability, and on-time project execution.

01

Requirement Analysis

Thorough evaluation of design constraints, functional loads, operating environments, physical mating surfaces, and target materials.

02

Concept Development

Preliminary 3D architecture, spatial allocation, mechanism topology, and initial feasibility reviews.

03

3D CAD Modeling

Parametric solid modeling in SOLIDWORKS or CATIA V5 with fully constrained sketches, standard feature hierarchies, and mates.

04

Engineering Validation

Dynamic interference checks, clearance audits, tolerance stack-up analysis, and assembly ergonomic verification.

05

DFM / Optimization

Optimization for manufacturing tooling access, laser-cutting K-factors, press-brake bend deductions, and weight reduction.

06

Technical Documentation

Creation of detailed 2D production blueprints to ASME Y14.5 with datum callouts, GD&T, weld symbols, and automated BOMs.

07

Prototype / Manufacturing Support

Generation of universal neutral files (STEP, IGES, DXF, STL for 3D printing) and ongoing technical coordination during initial fabrication.
