Methodology

Industry-Ready Engineering Workflow

A rigorous 7-stage engineering methodology guaranteeing dimensional precision, cost efficiency, and flawless manufacturing execution.

01

Requirement Analysis

Phase 01

Review design inputs, operational loads, materials, environmental conditions, and manufacturing constraints.

02

Concept Development

Phase 02

Brainstorm preliminary geometry, kinematic mechanisms, component layout, and structural load paths.

03

3D CAD Modeling

Phase 03

Build robust, parameter-driven solid and surface models in SOLIDWORKS or CATIA V5 with clean feature trees.

04

Engineering Validation

Phase 04

Perform interference detection, assembly mate checks, tolerance stack-up analysis, and motion simulations.

05

DFM / Optimization

Phase 05

Optimize design for laser cutting, press brake bend deductions, casting draft angles, and CNC milling tooling access.

06

Technical Documentation

Phase 06

Generate production blueprints adhering to ASME Y14.5 GD&T, surface finishes, weld symbols, and automated BOM.

07

Prototype & Manufacturing Support

Phase 07

Export 1:1 laser DXFs, neutral STEP/IGES/STL files, and assist shop-floor teams through prototype assembly.
